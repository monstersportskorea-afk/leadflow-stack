Google Ads can generate the lead. The next five minutes decide the outcome.

A practical response system should connect form submissions or inbound calls to a CRM, notify the right person, and create a short follow-up path when nobody responds immediately.

Lead eventAutomation goal
Form submittedCreate contact, tag source, notify owner, offer next step.
Call missedAcknowledge quickly and create a callback task.
Appointment bookedStop prospecting messages and move to reminder sequence.
No responseUse a limited follow-up sequence with clear exit rules.
Lead repliesEscalate to a human and stop automated chasing.

What to measure

  • Median time to first response
  • Contact rate
  • Appointment rate
  • Lead-to-opportunity rate
  • Booked-job or closed-deal rate when the client can share it

These metrics are more useful than judging campaign quality only by cost per lead.
