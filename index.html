Why agencies lose value after generating the lead

Problem 1

Calls go unanswered

A prospect calls while the business is busy. Without an immediate recovery workflow, intent cools quickly.

Problem 2

Follow-up is manual

Staff rely on memory, spreadsheets, or scattered inboxes instead of a repeatable workflow.

Problem 3

The agency gets blamed

Lead generation can look ineffective when the real failure happens after the lead arrives.

The workflow to test

StepRecommended action
1. Missed call detectedTrigger an automated workflow when a call is missed.
2. Immediate acknowledgmentSend a short, context-appropriate reply that makes it easy to continue the conversation.
3. CRM recordCreate or update the contact so the lead does not disappear into a personal inbox.
4. Follow-up sequenceRoute the lead into a simple, limited follow-up sequence with stop conditions.
5. Human handoffNotify staff when a lead replies or reaches a high-intent stage.

Who this is best for

Agencies serving appointment-driven local businesses such as HVAC, plumbing, roofing, dental, med-spa, automotive, legal intake, and other service categories where inbound calls matter.
