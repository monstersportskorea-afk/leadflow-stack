HighLevel Google Business Profile Call Tracking + Missed Call Text Back

A practical map for connecting Google Business Profile calls to a HighLevel number, identifying missed calls and starting a controlled follow-up.

Short answer

Use a HighLevel tracking or business number as the call destination shown through the supported Google Business Profile setup. When that number records an incoming missed call, the call details workflow can apply the follow-up rules and send one appropriate acknowledgment.

The call path

Google Business Profile

The profile presents the business call action and sends the caller to the configured destination.

HighLevel number

The destination number records the incoming call and its outcome in the intended location.

Follow-up workflow

The missed-call trigger checks eligibility, sends the message and hands off replies.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Choose the business location and document the current public call number and routing.
  2. Configure the supported HighLevel number and forwarding path for the intended account.
  3. Update the Google Business Profile call destination only after the receiving path is ready to test.
  4. Place test calls and verify that HighLevel records them as Incoming with the expected status.
  5. Build the missed-call workflow with filters for location, consent, active sequences and business hours.
  6. Send one short acknowledgment and define what happens when the caller replies.
  7. Test answered, busy, no-answer and voicemail paths before handing the setup to the client.

Common mistakes

  • Changing the public number before confirming forwarding and recording.
  • Testing only an answered call, which never exercises the missed-call branch.
  • Mixing several locations or phone numbers in one unfiltered workflow.
  • Assuming call tracking alone proves that a follow-up message was delivered.
  • Skipping disclosure, consent, opt-out and regional messaging checks.

FAQ

Does a Google Business Profile call automatically create a missed-call text?

Only when the call reaches the configured HighLevel number, is recorded with the expected status and passes the workflow's eligibility rules.

Should the original public number be removed?

Do not change public routing until the replacement path has been tested and documented for the business.

How can an agency debug a missing call?

Compare the profile destination, forwarding result, HighLevel call record, location and workflow execution history in that order.
