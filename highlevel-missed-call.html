A practical missed-call recovery blueprint

The goal is not to spam every caller. The goal is to create a controlled recovery workflow that acknowledges an unanswered call and gives the prospect a clear next step.

Trigger

Use a missed-call or call-status event appropriate to the account setup.

Reply

Send a short message identifying the business and offering a simple path to continue.

Escalation

If the lead replies, stop automated chasing and route the conversation to a human.

Agency checklist before enabling it

  • Confirm consent and messaging rules for the jurisdiction.
  • Use business identification in messages.
  • Set quiet hours and sensible retry limits.
  • Define stop conditions when the lead responds or opts out.
  • Test from a real phone before enabling on a client account.
